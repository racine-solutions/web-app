Release Notes (Version 1.15.2)

New Features

  • Mobile App (Self-Service): Clients can use the Racine mobile app to see their accounts, transfer money and request loans and savings accounts.
  • Mobile App Sign-up and Forgot Password: 6-digit codes sent by SMS to the phone on the client record (email as backup). Clients log in with their username, email or phone number.
  • Self Service Users: New Admin screen to manage mobile app users: view only, suspend, reactivate, close, unlock, sign out of all devices and reset password. Each change needs a reason, and the client is told by SMS and email.
  • Mobile App Transfer Limits and Linked Clients: Per-currency limits for each transfer and for each day, and the clients a mobile app user can act for.
  • Mobile App Activity Timeline: Each mobile app user's logins, transfers, access changes and refused transfers.
  • Mobile App Reports: Adoption by office, sign-up progress and mobile app transactions, with CSV export (Reports > Mobile App).
  • Mobile App Settings: Switch the whole mobile app on or off (switching it off signs everyone out), set the inactivity timeout (15 minutes by default) and the support phone numbers shown to blocked clients.
  • Loan Clearance SMS: Clients get an SMS when their loan is fully paid or overpaid.
  • Savings, Deposit and Share SMS: SMS for savings, fixed deposit, recurring deposit and share accounts: opening, approval, activation, deposits and share purchases, withdrawals and share redemptions. Each can be switched on or off in SMS Event Configuration.
  • SMS Status Banner: Staff see an amber banner when SMS credit runs low and a red one when it is almost used up. The alert levels can be changed in Configurations. A red banner also shows when SMS is switched off and clients are not being notified.
  • SMS Wallet: Shows the percentage of SMS credit used.
  • Sms Configuration: SMS notification and template management systems.
  • Dashboard Analytics: Custom stats component showing active loans, interest, penalties, and arrears statistics.
  • Configurable Analytics Dashboard: Configurable metrics and charts foundation for dashboard.
  • Reporting Dashboard: Interactive dashboard displaying client and collection trends.
  • Credit Profile Tab: Credit Profile section in the client details view.
  • Bureau Readiness Tab: Bureau Readiness verification tab in client details.
  • Working Capital Loans: Support for pause/resume delinquency classification, charge adjustments, and originator configuration.
  • Communication Campaigns: Send SMS and email campaigns to the clients you choose, under RacinePay Premium > Campaigns. Messages are filled in for each client (name, loan balance, next due date, savings, shares and more), and every SMS is checked to fit one message.
  • Campaign Approval: Every campaign is submitted for approval before anything is sent, and can be rejected with a reason. Maker-checker applies when it is switched on.
  • Campaign Scheduling: Send now, at a set time, or repeatedly (every few days, weeks or months) until a date or a number of runs. Quiet hours (08:00–20:00 by default) keep messages to daytime; campaigns can be paused, resumed or stopped.
  • Campaign Delivery Reports: Each run shows who was sent, failed or skipped and why, with retry for failed messages and CSV export. Clients without a phone or email, or sharing one with another client, are skipped.
  • Email Campaigns: Email templates with a subject and a plain or designed (HTML) body. Reports show which emails were delivered, bounced or marked as spam. A daily email limit (250 by default) keeps room for passwords and codes; emails over it go out the next day.
  • Email Sender Name: Each institution can set the name its clients see as the sender of its emails (Configurations > email-sender-name).
  • Marketing Opt-out: Staff can record on a client's profile that they do not want sales and marketing messages; marketing campaigns then skip them. The profile also lists the campaign messages each client received.
  • Campaign Audiences: Build an audience on its own page in three steps: choose the filters, review the matching clients and leave out anyone who should not get the message, then name and save it. Saved audiences can be edited, their clients viewed, and used to start a campaign.
  • Campaign Template Guidance: The template editor explains how SMS and email messages work, offers ready-made examples to start from (repayment reminder, overdue loan, savings balance, birthday wishes, account summary and loan offer emails) and shows a live preview as you type.
  • Staff Audiences: Campaigns can go to your own staff and system users, not only clients. Choose them by staff type (loan officers, with or without a system login), office, role or name, or pick staff who are also clients. Staff messages can include each officer's own figures: their clients, active loans and loans in arrears. A campaign to staff cannot use client details, and one to clients cannot use staff details.
  • More Audience Filters: Reach clients by loan officer, group, years as a member, phone or email on record, loan balance, days in arrears, loans ending or disbursed recently, number of loans taken, pending loan applications, loan purpose, savings product, no savings deposit for a while, or whether they are also staff.
  • More Message Details: Templates can include a client's last name, email, loan officer and member since date, their loan product, masked loan and savings account numbers, last payment and last deposit, loan end date, deposit balances and today's date.
  • Email Building Blocks: The email editor can format text (lists, underline, highlight, centre) and insert ready-made pieces: a button, a notice box, a table of the client's figures, a divider, an image, a signature and a footer.

Bug Fixes

  • Dashboard: The Analytics Report now shows when the dashboard opens, without switching tabs first.
  • SMS and Transactions: An SMS problem, such as no SMS credit left, no longer stops a repayment, deposit or approval from going through.
  • Group Accounts: Transactions on group accounts no longer fail when SMS is switched on.
  • Mobile App Security: Confirmed transfers now check that the account belongs to the client, and a password change in the app only changes that app user's password.
  • Blocked Mobile App Users: Suspended, closed or locked users are signed out at once instead of when their session ran out.
  • Mobile App Beneficiaries: Fixed the beneficiary list failing to load.
  • Route to loan repayment at the dollar sign in client/group profile.
  • Fix all pentaho reports to be able to export in csv, excell, normal format.
  • 2FA Authentication: Resolved 2FA email OTP not working during login.
  • Identifier Images: Fixed rendering of identifier images after refresh.
  • Datatable Scrolling: Restored horizontal scrolling on custom datatable views.
  • Formula Injection Prevention: Sanitized CSV/XLSX exports to prevent formula injection.
  • Templates ID: Ensured template ID is included on template updates.
  • Client and Loan Screen Reports: Generating a screen report from a template now shows the report filled in for the client or loan, instead of the template itself.

Improvements

  • Shorter, Safer SMS: Every SMS fits in one message, greets the client by name, shows account numbers masked (e.g. 0****13) and ends with the institution's name when there is room.
  • Mobile App SMS: Mobile app messages and codes now go out through the same SMS service as account messages.
  • Shared Contact Details: A client whose phone number or email is also on another client's record is kept out of the mobile app until staff correct it, so codes cannot reach the wrong person.
  • Enhance on dashboard reports.
  • Datatable Data Refreshing: Automatically refresh datatable tab data on route updates.
  • Localization and Translation: Improved loan product creation localization and external event labels translations.
  • Autocomplete Results: Refresh transfer client autocomplete results on demand.
  • Campaign Audience Step: A campaign now reaches a saved audience chosen from a list, so who it reaches is set in one place and a campaign can no longer go to every client by mistake.
  • SMS and Email Templates: The SMS and the email version of a template are kept apart while editing, so an email is never checked against the SMS length. Names are suggested, not filled in.
  • Campaigns Replace SMS Campaigns: The old SMS Campaigns screen under Organization is replaced by RacinePay Premium > Campaigns, and campaign templates no longer appear among the screen report templates.
